The Four Pillars

What does I.D.E.A mean?

Four ideas. One belief — that the world is better when everyone belongs. Each pillar is a commitment we make to ourselves, to each other, and to our communities.

I
Inclusion
🤝

"There is always room for one more."

Inclusion means making sure that every person — regardless of their background, beliefs, or abilities — feels truly welcome. It's not just about being invited in. It's about belonging.

D
Diversity
🎨

"Our differences are our greatest strength."

Diversity is the beautiful reality that no two people are the same. Different languages, traditions, experiences, and perspectives don't divide us — they make us richer together.

E
Equity
⚖️

"Fair doesn't always mean the same."

Equity means giving every person what they actually need to thrive — not the same thing, but the right thing. When we lift others up, everyone rises.

A
Awareness
💡

"Change begins when we choose to see."

Awareness is opening our eyes — and hearts — to the experiences of others. It's asking questions, listening deeply, and recognising that the world looks different depending on where you stand.

Why It Matters

The world needs I.D.E.A now more than ever

Children who grow up understanding inclusion, diversity, equity, and awareness don't just become better students — they become better humans. The need has never been clearer.

🏫
1 in 3

Students report regularly feeling like they don't truly belong at school — a feeling that affects their confidence, learning, and wellbeing.

🌍
7,000+

Languages are spoken around the world — yet most children grow up only ever learning in one. Language is identity, and every identity deserves to be seen.

💛
Proven

Research consistently shows that children who feel included perform better, build stronger friendships, and grow into more empathetic, resilient adults.

Meet the Creator

Aria Kottaka Raj

Author · Advocate · Student · Changemaker

"I believe every child deserves to feel seen, heard, and included — and that books and technology are two of the most powerful ways to make that happen."

Hi, I'm Aria! I'm a student at The Village School in Houston, Texas, and I wrote my first book when I was 9 years old. I live with my mom, dad, and my fluffy sidekick Muffin. Growing up at an international school taught me that diversity isn't just something to tolerate — it's something to celebrate.

I created CultureReads because I saw families in my community struggling to understand letters, notices, and documents in a language they were still learning. And I wrote I.D.E.A Superstar because I believed every child deserved a book that spoke directly to them about belonging. This platform is my way of putting those beliefs into action — and I'm just getting started.
