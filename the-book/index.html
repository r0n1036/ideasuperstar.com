About the Book

A book that teaches kids to change the world

I.D.E.A Superstar was written by Aria Kottaka Raj at just 9 years old — born from a simple but powerful belief that books have the power to bring people together, spark important conversations, and teach us all something about who we want to be.

Through age-appropriate language, the book breaks down four essential concepts — giving children and families the language to understand inclusion, diversity, equity, and awareness, and opening conversations that matter at home, in classrooms, and in communities.

What Does I.D.E.A Mean?

Four big ideas. One young author.

Each letter represents a concept that helps children understand how to build a kinder, fairer, more inclusive world.

I
Inclusion
🤝

Being included and making sure everyone feels welcome — regardless of talents, beliefs, or background. An includer always thinks of others.

D
Diversity
🎨

We are all unique — different languages, traditions, backgrounds. A diversity champion embraces every difference as something to celebrate.

E
Equity
⚖️

Giving everyone what they need to succeed — not the same thing, but the right thing. Everyone deserves fair access to opportunities.

A
Awareness
💡

It is up to all of us to create a fair world — to look out for each other and think about how others experience life around us.

Inside the Book

Stories children will remember

⚽
The Soccer Field

"I also want to play soccer." "Sure, there's always room for you to join!" A simple moment that teaches inclusion in action.

🍎
The Apple Tree

Equity is recognizing that not everyone can reach apples with the same ladder. Everyone needs to be treated fairly and given real opportunities.

🌱
The Plant Analogy

Not all plants need the same amount of water. If we give every plant the same, some will thrive, some will be thirsty, and some will drown.
